KnitSnap

An iOS app for logging your knitting progress with photo overlays of project details.

About

KnitSnap lets you document the progress of your knitting projects with photos, and compose clean overlays of project name, needles, pattern, and yarn directly onto them. All data is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server.
